# Creator Radar privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Creator Radar is a personal desktop application. Its owner uses it to research public YouTube data and to view analytics for their own YouTube channels.

## Data the app accesses

When you connect a YouTube channel, Creator Radar asks Google for read-only access (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly and yt-analytics-monetary.readonly). With that access it reads your channel and video details and your YouTube Analytics reports, such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, audience demographics and, for monetised channels, estimated revenue.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to show reports, charts and recommendations inside Creator Radar. It is not sold, shared with advertisers or used for advertising.

If you choose to use the optional Ask Claude feature, the reports you ask about are sent to Anthropic's Claude through your own Claude account so Claude can answer your question. Nothing is sent to Claude unless you use that feature.

## Where the data is stored

Everything is stored on your own computer in the app's local database. Google sign-in tokens are encrypted with Windows data protection. Creator Radar has no servers of its own; the app talks directly to Google and YouTube.

## Removing data and access

Disconnect a channel in the app (My Channels) or delete the app's data folder to remove its data. You can revoke Creator Radar's access to your Google account at any time at [Google account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Google and YouTube

Creator Radar uses YouTube API Services. See the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Creator Radar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
