# Creator Radar

Creator Radar is a personal desktop app for YouTube research and channel analytics. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used to study public YouTube data and to view analytics for the owner's own YouTube channels.

## What it does

- Researches public YouTube data (videos, channels, view counts, transcripts) to find content ideas and niches.
- With your permission, reads YouTube Analytics for channels you connect (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources) so you can see how your videos perform.
- Access to your channel is read-only. Creator Radar never uploads, edits or deletes anything on YouTube.

Creator Radar uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).
